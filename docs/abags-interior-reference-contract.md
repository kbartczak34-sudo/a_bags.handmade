# A-Bags atelier interior reference contract

The three supplied atelier photographs are the current visual reference set for the Digital Craft Twin. They establish appearance targets for the interior, lining, crochet rim, flap, handles, hardware and accessories; they do not prove unseen dimensions or backside geometry.

## Observed construction

- Fabric lining is recessed inside the crocheted body and follows the inner wall.
- The crochet upper rim is a thick continuous finished edge around the opening.
- Interior depth remains visible from the customer camera.
- The brand label is a sewn rectangular fabric label on the inner wall.
- Hardware and accessory elements remain visually separate from textile geometry.

## Reference variants

### Red / dark wood
- saturated red crochet body;
- dark brown polished wood handles;
- cream lining with red/brown plaid grid;
- warm gold hardware;
- printed cream/blush/red bow;
- wide woven shoulder strap with red, cream and tan bands.

### Mauve / light wood
- mauve/purple crochet body;
- honey-colored wood handles;
- cream lining with red/brown plaid grid;
- light leather details and warm metal accents;
- pale butterfly ornament;
- contrasting decorative band across the front.

### Cream / pink
- cream crochet body;
- pink smooth flap with stitched perimeter;
- central gold-tone clasp;
- cream floral lining with pink flowers and green foliage;
- cream braided/crocheted upper rim;
- gold side attachment hardware;
- pink and cream tassel.

## Modeling requirements

1. Interior wall and lining are separate geometry/materials.
2. Flap is a separate mesh with visible thickness and stitched edge.
3. Handles are separate meshes with physically plausible attachment points.
4. Hardware is separate from textile geometry.
5. Accessories are independently addressable by the configurator.
6. Crochet relief is represented by validated geometry and/or normal/height detail; a blurred photographic texture alone is insufficient.
7. Hidden surfaces are not promoted as production truth until validated.

## Promotion rule

A GLB can enter the production Digital Craft Twin only after exterior, opening, lining, flap, handle attachment, hardware and accessory placement pass visual QA against the supplied physical references.
