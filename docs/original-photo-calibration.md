# Original-photo calibration — 29 October 2026

The owner supplied `1000019763.jpg` (chocolate crossbody) and
`1000019645.jpg` (green/turquoise/blue wooden-handle tote). The existing
360 px illustrated thumbnails are 3D previews, not those original photographs.

The active WebGL classic stitch uses longer, straighter V legs and approximately
twelve columns across the body, with the same gauge on the gusset and crocheted
flap. The back mirrors the front. Cord diameter, deterministic hand tension,
local contact occlusion and directional fibre lighting are preserved. The other
three stitch constructions retain their own paths and gauges.

The chocolate crossbody has a taller front silhouette (width/height ratio about
1.25 instead of 1.37 in renderer units), a broader bow placed farther left and
a wider braided shoulder strap. Its opening, zipper and side attachments follow
the new body height. The ocean tote has broader bow wings. Both reference badges
sit higher; the chocolate badge also moves right to clear the hanging strap.

These are visual proportions from a single photographed viewpoint, not new
physical measurements. Only the approximate 16 cm body width is confirmed;
height and depth remain unmeasured. The original upload paths were unavailable
in the execution environment, so the JPGs are not added as repository assets.
The existing fabric textures remain reference-derived approximations rather
than pixel-identical copies of the photographed scarves. Do not call the result
a verified 1:1 digital twin.
